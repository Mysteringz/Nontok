GUIDED FIELD SURVEYProbe & measure the fieldNOT STARTED

The Mega averages the measurements and fits the walls and beacon positions. Keep motors disarmed: rotate or reposition the robot by hand, then let it settle before each capture. Use a tape measure for the sensor mounts and reference floor poses. The routine cannot detect the inked parking-box boundaries.

Connect the Mega to begin.0 / 40 captures

1. Measure sensor mounts

Offsets from chassis centre to each ultrasonic face, in the robot’s own frame. Forward and left are positive; right is negative. Beam angles are fixed at ±45°.

2. Start at a known pose

Place the chassis centre at measured X/Y. Set its known world heading; 180° faces the back wall. Starting clears the previous scan. Rotate in place by hand in roughly 15° steps, capturing after each stop.

3. Optional light fitting

Turn the three beacons off for an ambient capture, then turn them back on. Enter measured height differences between the light receiver and each beacon. Exponent 2 is a model assumption to validate.

For light fitting, use at least six distinct measured positions spread in X and Y, all facing the same heading. A nine-point grid is recommended.

Stop the robot before capturing. Calibration runs on the Mega and is saved to its EEPROM with Save to Mega. After every reboot, set the robot’s known world heading. Confirm sensor models and wiring before enabling the firmware drivers.

1. Heading & paper

Place the stopped robot at a measured orientation. 0° faces +X; +90° faces +Y. The underfront colour sensor must be over the paper when teaching the centre pose.

Colour references: waiting

2. Independent beacon signals

Analog strength mode needs three separately resolved channels and measured signal-to-distance calibration. Ordinary combined brightness cannot be used. Measure each beacon’s X/Y position: the side lights appear forward of the back wall. The 1,500 mm measurement is wall spacing, not a common beacon line. Enter ambient-subtracted reference signal at a measured slant distance. Verify the optical model at several positions and headings.

Side beacon positions await measurement.

3. Centre, turns & boxes

Capture a base pose, then samples from that same base displaced by a measured +X, +Y or CCW angle. Return to the base before each offset. Keep the same reflectors. Base capture clears that point’s old derivatives.

Use 20–80 mm for X/Y; 2–10° for yaw. Turn references stay at the centre X/Y with headings exactly centre ±90°. Box positions remain yours to provide.
